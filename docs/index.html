Clip Factory

Clip Factory is a personal, non-commercial tool. It finds the best moments in long gameplay videos, turns them into short vertical clips with captions, and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads short videos to the owner's own channel, with a title, description and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads view and like counts, and the viewer country breakdown, for those uploads.

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to anyone else.
